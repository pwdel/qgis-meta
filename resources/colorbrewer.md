# ColorBrewer

- Publisher: ColorBrewer project.
- Category: cartographic palettes and guidance.
- Source: [Color scheme types](https://colorbrewer2.org/learnmore/schemes_full.html).
- Review state: pointer checked; checked 2026-10-09.
- Checked scope: palette-selection guidance; palette redistribution license not assessed.

The guidance distinguishes sequential palettes for ordered values, diverging palettes around a meaningful midpoint, and qualitative palettes for categories. Use these distinctions when selecting a scheme for the mapped variable.

Potential uses: thematic layers in any starter profile. Check the specific palette's reuse terms before bundling it. Test the actual legend and output; a palette choice alone does not establish accessibility. See [use of color](wcag-use-of-color.md).
