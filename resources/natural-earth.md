# Natural Earth

- Publisher: Natural Earth contributors.
- Category: downloadable vector and raster basemap data.
- Source and terms: [Terms of Use](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).
- Review state: conditions summarized; checked 2026-10-09.

Natural Earth states its raster and vector map data are public domain. Its terms allow modification, electronic distribution, and printing, including commercial use; credit is optional. “Made with Natural Earth” is an available credit.

The site describes datasets at 1:10 million, 1:50 million, and 1:110 million scales. Editorial suitability: regional/world reference maps and overview insets. These generalized datasets should not be selected as detailed trail-navigation evidence.

Interactive, print, and offline use of the downloaded data are supported by the stated terms. Check the chosen dataset's version, coverage, and accuracy. These data terms do not imply rights to an unrelated hosting service.
