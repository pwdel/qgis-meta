# NPS pictographic symbols

- Publisher: U.S. National Park Service, NPMap.
- Category: recreation and visitor-information symbols.
- Source: [Pictographic Symbols](https://www.nps.gov/maps/web/tools/park-tiles/pictographic-symbols/index.html).
- Review state: pointer checked; checked 2026-10-09.
- Checked scope: web-map symbol library; individual download licenses and attribution not assessed.

The library presents NPS web-map pictographs adapted from Harpers Ferry Center map symbols. It is a discovery source for recreation points of interest.

Potential use: trail and visitor maps. Review the exact downloadable package, license, format, and output suitability before importing or redistributing symbols. Print and offline use remain unresolved here. Symbol use does not establish NPS endorsement or applicability of NPS standards to a non-NPS map.
