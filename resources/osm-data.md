# OpenStreetMap data

- Publisher: OpenStreetMap Foundation and contributors.
- Category: geographic data.
- Source and terms: [Copyright and License](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
- Review state: conditions summarized; checked 2026-10-09.
- Checked scope: data licensing and attribution overview, not every downstream distribution scenario.

OSM data is offered under ODbL. Provide the required contributor credit and license notice. Database adaptation and distribution can carry additional obligations; check the full license for the actual product. Do not infer that every rendered map must use the database's license.

The data license is distinct from a tile provider's service terms. See [standard raster tiles](osm-standard-tiles.md) for that specific endpoint.

Potential uses: trail context, habitat context, and general reference. Local completeness, currency, scale, and access claims require separate assessment. Interactive, print, and offline products each need an appropriate data distribution and attribution review; this entry does not authorize any particular download service.
