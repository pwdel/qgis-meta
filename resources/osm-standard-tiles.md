# OpenStreetMap standard raster tiles

- Publisher: OpenStreetMap Foundation.
- Category: hosted basemap.
- Endpoint in scope: `https://tile.openstreetmap.org/{z}/{x}/{y}.png`.
- Terms: [Tile Usage Policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/).
- Review state: conditions summarized; checked 2026-10-09.

Normal interactive viewing is supported subject to visible attribution, client identification, caching, and other published requirements. Bulk downloading, prefetching, and offline packaging are prohibited for this endpoint. Service availability is best effort.

For offline maps, select another source whose terms permit that use. Print/export permission and the retrieval method require a separate use-specific check. These restrictions concern this service, not all OSM data or all OSM-derived providers. Vector tiles have a separate policy.

Related: [OSM data](osm-data.md). Potential use: online context across the starter profiles, after checking client compliance.
