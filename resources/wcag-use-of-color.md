# WCAG: use of color

- Publisher: W3C.
- Category: web accessibility standard and explanatory guidance.
- Standard: [WCAG 2.2, Success Criterion 1.4.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#use-of-color).
- Explanation: [Understanding Use of Color](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/use-of-color.html).
- Review state: conditions summarized; checked 2026-10-09.
- Checked scope: this criterion, not a full WCAG assessment.

For web content, color must not be the sole visual means of communicating information. Labels, shapes, and patterns can provide additional cues. The Understanding document explains the criterion; it is not itself the normative standard.

Potential uses: every profile's web output. Applying similar checks to printed maps is a local editorial recommendation. This criterion alone does not establish full accessibility or legal compliance, and this entry does not assess reproduction licenses for W3C documents.
