# Place and activity evidence

Status: local editorial policy, established 2026-10-09. This page contains no jurisdiction-specific legal determinations.

For trail and habitat-scouting maps, keep observed features, predicted habitat, ownership evidence, and activity permission distinct. A trail line, park boundary, or habitat polygon alone is not permission to enter or undertake an activity.

When a project includes an access claim, link the responsible authority's evidence and state the place, activity, date, conditions, and remaining uncertainty. Keep missing or conflicting evidence unresolved. Current closures and site restrictions need a project-specific source.

An external governing-rules registry may be linked when one is selected and its scope and provenance are established. This first edition selects no jurisdictional registry and makes no parcel-level permission claims.

For habitat outputs, separately review whether precise observations should be publicly disclosed. This is an editorial publication decision and must not be presented as a universal legal rule.
