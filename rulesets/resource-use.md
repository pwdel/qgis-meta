# Resource use and attribution

Reviewed 2026-10-09. Applies to resources selected for any starter map profile.

| Rule | Origin | Scope and evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Record the exact item, version, provider, and output mode before deciding use conditions | Local editorial policy | Every selection; a catalog entry is a starting point |
| Meet OSM data credit and license obligations | Publisher license | Products using [OSM data](../resources/osm-data.md); follow the linked terms for the specific distribution |
| Follow attribution, client, and caching requirements; do not prefetch or package the standard raster service for offline use | Publisher service policy | Only the endpoint in [OSM standard raster tiles](../resources/osm-standard-tiles.md) |
| Natural Earth data may be reused under its public-domain terms; credit is optional | Publisher terms | Data covered by [Natural Earth](../resources/natural-earth.md) |
| Treat unspecified print/offline rights as unresolved | Local editorial policy | Do not extrapolate from interactive access or a downloadable preview |
| Preserve attribution in the final output | Local editorial policy supporting source requirements | Check both exported layouts and interactive views against the selected resource's actual terms |

A local exception can adjust a local policy. It cannot override a publisher's license or service conditions. Record exceptions and their scope in the map project's README.
